▫️ **Lesson 1: hello, please, thank you**

1. **Guten Morgen** (GOO-ten MOR-gen): good morning. Use it until about eleven.
2. **Guten Tag**: hello. This is the safe greeting for the rest of the day.
3. **Hallo**: hi. Friendly, and fine with other exhibitors.
4. **Danke** / **Danke schön**: thanks / thank you very much.
5. **Bitte**: please. It also means "you're welcome", which is handy.
6. **Entschuldigung**: excuse me, or sorry.
7. **Auf Wiedersehen** / **Tschüss**: goodbye, formal and casual.

▫️ **Sie and du**

German has two words for "you". **Sie** is polite, for strangers and anyone selling you something. **du** is for friends. From what I've read, people at game fairs move to **du** quickly. I'd still start with **Sie** and follow the other person's lead. Nobody minds being treated politely.
