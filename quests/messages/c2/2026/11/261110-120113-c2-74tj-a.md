▫️ **Lesson 4: numbers and prices**

1. **zehn, zwanzig, dreißig, vierzig, fünfzig**: ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty.
2. **hundert**: a hundred.
3. **Was kostet das?** What does it cost?
4. **Das kostet fünfundzwanzig.** It costs twenty-five.
5. **Ab welcher Stückzahl gibt es einen Rabatt?** From what quantity is there a discount?
6. **Haben Sie eine Visitenkarte?** Do you have a business card?

▫️ **The tricky bit**

German says the ones before the tens: 25 is **fünfundzwanzig**, "five-and-twenty". 42 is **zweiundvierzig**, "two-and-forty". When someone says a price, the last part you hear is the tens, so wait for the end before you decide. I'd write the number down rather than trust your ear on the first day. Fair stands usually have a pen.

▫️ **Practice**

Read five prices off any receipt aloud, in German.\
