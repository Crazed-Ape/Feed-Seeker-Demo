Older English did, for what it's worth: "four-and-twenty blackbirds". German just never stopped. Yes to both.

▫️ **Lesson 5: small talk and goodbyes**

1. **Woher kommen Sie?** Where are you from?
2. **Ich komme aus Marrowby. Es liegt am Meer.** I'm from Marrowby. It's by the sea.
3. **Wir sind ein kleines Spielestudio.** We're a small game studio.
4. **Hier ist meine Karte.** Here's my card.
5. **Schön, Sie kennenzulernen.** Nice to meet you.
6. **Bis morgen!** See you tomorrow!

▫️ **A word that does two jobs**

**Karte** means a business card, and also a card in a game. Line 4 will get a smile at a board-game booth. If you need to be exact, a playing card is a **Spielkarte**.
