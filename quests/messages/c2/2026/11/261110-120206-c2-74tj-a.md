▫️ **Review 1: Sie and du**

With **Sie**, the verb ends in **-en**: **Möchten Sie**, **Kommen Sie**. With **du**, it ends in **-st**: **Möchtest du**, **Kommst du**. Your 3 and 9 mixed them. For polite, look for **-en**.

▫️ **Review 2: numbers backwards**

**zweiundfünfzig** is 52, so you said the numbers the English way round. In German the ones come first, then **und**, then the tens:

1. 25: **fünf-und-zwanzig**.
2. 52: **zwei-und-fünfzig**.

▫️ **Review 3: the right rescue phrase**

**Langsamer, bitte** asks for slower, which is useful but answers a different question. For "Pardon?", the short one is **Wie bitte?**

▫️ **And number 10**

**Schön, Sie kennenzulernen** is the full version. There's also a shortcut: **Freut mich!** means "pleased to meet you", and it works with anyone, **Sie** or **du**.
