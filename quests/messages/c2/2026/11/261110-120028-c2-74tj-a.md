Very normal. Long words come back with use, not with staring at them. **aus-pro-BEE-ren**, "try out". Today's lesson is the one that rescues you when words don't come back.

▫️ **Lesson 3: when you don't understand**

1. **Wie bitte?** Sorry? / Pardon? The quickest one.
2. **Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?** Could you repeat that, please?
3. **Langsamer, bitte.** Slower, please.
4. **Ich verstehe nicht.** I don't understand.
5. **Ich spreche nur ein bisschen Deutsch.** I only speak a little German.
6. **Sprechen Sie Englisch?** Do you speak English?

▫️ **Why this one matters**

In my reading on phrasebook learners, the common trouble isn't saying things. It's the reply, which comes back fast and full. Line 5 gets most people to slow down kindly. Line 3 does the rest.

Line 2 is a mouthful, so it's today's practice.\
