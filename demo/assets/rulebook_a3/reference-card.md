## Front · Your turn, in four steps

1. **Move:** up to two docks along the path, either way, or stay.
2. **Take or trade:** take one card from the tide line, or swap one card with a neighbor at your dock.
3. **Work:** play cards that match empty spaces on the task at your dock. One shell per card. Five shells finish a task.
4. **Rest:** more than five cards? Discard down to five.

Talk at any time. It's the whole game.

## Back · One turn, start to finish

1. The Baker reaches the Fish Market. Mend the Nets needs a net and two ropes; the Baker holds a net and one rope.
2. The tide line has no rope, but the Net Mender is at the same dock with two.
3. Take or trade: the Baker trades a bread for one of the Net Mender's ropes.
4. Work: the Baker plays the net and both ropes, and places three shells.
5. Five shells: the nets are finished. Turn the task sideways; the tide can't touch it now.
