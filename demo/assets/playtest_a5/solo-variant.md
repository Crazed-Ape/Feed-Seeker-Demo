# {{pick.game}}: Solo Variant

One player, about 25 minutes. Everything not mentioned here works as in the full game.

## Setup

1. Unfold the board and lay out the market row of five cards, as usual.
2. Take one player's tokens and the lantern.
3. Deal yourself a hand of task cards, as in a two-player game.
4. Deal eight more task cards face up beside the board. These are the village's tasks.
5. Put 8 favor tokens beside the board as the village's store. Return the rest to the box.
6. Shuffle the weather deck and place it in the empty seat on your left. This is your Neighbor.

## Your Turn

Play your turn exactly as in the full game. Favor you earn goes into the village's store. Favor you spend comes out of it. When you finish a task from the village's row, turn it face down.

## The Neighbor's Turn

The weather deck plays as a second neighbor who never helps and never complains.

1. Pass the lantern to the Neighbor.
2. Flip the top weather card and resolve it as if a second player had drawn it.
3. The Neighbor takes the leftmost market card and discards it. Refill the market row to five cards.
4. Pass the lantern back to yourself.

## Seasons

Play all four seasons: spring, summer, autumn and winter. A season ends when the weather deck shows its season-change card, as in the full game.

## Winning and Losing

Win: if every task is done by the end of winter, the village made it through and you win.

Loss: if the village runs out of favor, you lose.

## Target Time

25 minutes. Please note the time at each change of season.
