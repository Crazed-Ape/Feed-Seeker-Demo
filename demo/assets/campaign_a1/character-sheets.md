# The Dark Lighthouse: Character Sheets

Three heroes for one stormy night on the coast. Each sheet fits on its own page. When something is chancy, roll a twenty-sided die, add the trait your game master names, and say the total out loud.

---

## The Cartographer

*Maps places before they exist.*

| Trait | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Brains | +3 |
| Nerve | +0 |
| Snacks | +2 |

### Who they are

The Cartographer draws maps of places that haven't happened yet. Sometimes the places turn up later and look slightly embarrassed to be late. They carry eleven pencils at all times, sorted by mood, and they are not scared of heights, thank you very much. Anyone who mentions the word "ledge" will be ignored with great dignity.

### What they carry

1. Eleven pencils, one of them very short and very loved.
2. A roll of blank parchment.
3. A brass compass that always points to the nearest food. It has never once been wrong.

### Good at

Puzzles, directions, remembering the way back, and finding lunch.

## The Knight of Modest Means

*Excellent manners, no money.*

| Trait | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Strength | +3 |
| Charm | +2 |
| Wallet | −1 |

### Who she is

The Knight of Modest Means bows to innkeepers, holds doors for strangers, and apologizes to furniture she bumps into. She has never had two coins to rub together, so she pays for everything in buttons. Her coat has fewer buttons every week, and she thinks that's a fair price for good manners.

### What she carries

1. A sword, polished more often than it's used.
2. A helmet, slightly dented, very shiny.
3. A pouch of buttons: brass, bone, wood and one that might be silver.

### Good at

Lifting things, standing in front of things, and being so polite that people give in.

---

## The Herbalist

*Talks to plants, and is fairly sure they talk back.*

| Trait | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Remedies | +3 |
| Kindness | +2 |
| Balance | −1 |

### Who he is

The Herbalist greets every plant he passes and asks how it's getting on. Most of them, he says, are doing fine. Some of them complain. He suspects animals can understand him too, though so far none have admitted it. He is gentle, patient and not very good at staying upright on uneven ground.

### What he carries

1. A satchel of salves, seeds and dried things.
2. A small kettle, for teas of uncertain effect.
3. A hat he is quite attached to.

### Good at

Healing, soothing, coaxing, and making friends with anything that grows or breathes.

---

## For the whole table

1. Nobody has to lead. The game master asks the table, and the table answers together.
2. A roll of 10 or more is usually good news. A 1 is a story. A 20 is a bigger story.
3. Help each other. A hand to hold can be worth a bonus.
