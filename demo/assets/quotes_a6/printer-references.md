# Printer References: {{pick.printer_a}} and {{pick.printer_b}}

Notes by {{c2.name}} for {{c8.name}}, on how each printer has done for other small studios. No recommendation here; the cost side sits with {{c8.name}}.

## Sources

| Source | {{pick.printer_a}} | {{pick.printer_b}} |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier directory reviews (two directories) | 14 | 9 |

Also one long forum thread where small studios compare printers: 4 posts mention one of the two.

1. Nothing here is confirmed firsthand.
2. Reviews lean toward people who had a very good time or a very bad one, so the middle is probably quieter than it looks.
3. The samples are small. Read every count below as a hint, not a measurement.

## {{pick.printer_a}}

### Dates

1. Three of the 14 reviews mention a late delivery, each by about a week.
2. All three were orders in the last quarter of the year, which looks like their busy season.
3. The busy-season pattern comes from a single column in one of the directories (order month). Worth one direct question if the studio goes with them: "Is your schedule busier at this time of year?"

### Cards and tokens

1. Card and print quality are praised in 11 of the 14 reviews.
2. The wooden tokens are made in their own workshop. Two reviews mention clean cuts and even color.

### Boxes

1. No reports of boxes arriving damaged.

## {{pick.printer_b}}

### Dates

1. Dates were kept in 8 of the 9 reviews.
2. The ninth was held up by a freight company, not the printer.

### Cards and tokens

1. No complaints about card quality in the reviews found.
2. The tokens come from a partner workshop. One review says the colors varied between two batches.

### Boxes

1. One report, two years old, of box corners crushed in transit.
2. {{pick.printer_b}} answered it publicly and says the packing has changed since. That is their account; I found nothing newer either way.
