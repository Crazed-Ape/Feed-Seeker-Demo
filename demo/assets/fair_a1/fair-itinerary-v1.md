# Fair Trip: Itinerary, Version 1

Two people, two fair days in {{pick.fair_city}}: trade day on Wednesday, public day on Thursday. This file sets out both ways of getting there, hour by hour, so the choice can be made on the whole trip and not just the ticket price.

1. Setup: Wednesday, 8:00 to 10:30.
2. Trade day (shops and distributors): Wednesday, 10:30 to 18:00.
3. Public day (players): Thursday, 10:00 to 17:00. The hall closes at 17:00.
