# Backer Update Calendar, Version 2

Campaign for {{pick.game}}, 30 days. Six updates, revised after {{c6.name}}'s second look.

## The updates

| Campaign day | Update | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch post | The game, the campaign, and a thank-you to early backers while they are still arriving. |
| 3 | Behind the art | How the card art came together, with the first stretch-goal teaser. |
| 14 | Designer diary | Notes from {{p1.name}} on how the game was made, timed to break up the quiet middle. |
| 20 | Shipping timeline | Production and delivery plans, with the shipping given as a month, never a day. |
| 28 | The last 48 hours | Final reminder, stretch-goal progress, and how to change a pledge before the close. |
| 32 | What happens now | Two days after the close: surveys, the timeline from here, and a thank-you. |

## Changes from version 1

1. The day 4 thank-you is folded into the day 1 launch post.
2. The day 2 art post and the day 3 stretch-goal teaser are now one update on day 3.
3. The designer diary moves from day 10 to day 14, so days 11 to 19 aren't silent.
4. The day 20 shipping update gives a month, not a day, because the date depends on the printer's schedule.
5. A new update on day 32 covers what happens after the campaign closes.

## The one rule to keep

Every update gives a shipping month, never a shipping day.
