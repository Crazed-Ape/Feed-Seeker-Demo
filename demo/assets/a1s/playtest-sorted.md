## Theme 1: The Middle Rounds

Eight of twelve comments mention summer or autumn.

| Group | Count | Testers |
|---|---|---|
| Slow while choosing a task card | 5 | 1, 5, 6, 9, 12 |
| Slow, no cause given | 3 | 3, 8, 11 |
| Named the autumn harvest as a favorite | 4 | 1, 3, 11, 12 |
| Said they liked not being rushed | 2 | 7, 10 |

Notes:

1. Tester 9 names the cause directly: by summer, all five task cards in hand were playable.
2. The four harvest comments all sit inside the eight slow comments. People like autumn; they don't like how long it takes to get through it.
3. The two "not rushed" comments argue against anything with a clock in it.
