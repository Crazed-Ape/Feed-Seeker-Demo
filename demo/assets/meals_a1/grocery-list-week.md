# Grocery List: The Week

One shop on Sunday, for five dinners and a loose weekend. Three people. Sorted by aisle so the shop goes in one loop: start at produce, finish at the freezers.

Estimated total: about 90 at the usual shop.

Nothing on this list contains mushrooms. Check the labels on soups, sauces and pizza toppings too.

## 1. Produce

1. Potatoes, 1.5 kg (Monday)
2. Carrots, 750 g (Monday)
3. Peppers, 2, any color (Friday pizza)
4. Fresh produce for {{pick.dish}} (Wednesday, see section 7)

## 2. Bakery

1. Bread, 1 sliced loaf, sturdy enough for grilled cheese (Tuesday)

## 3. Meat counter

1. Chicken thighs, bone-in, 8 (Monday)
2. Sausages, 6 (Thursday). **Freeze these as soon as you get home.** Move them to the fridge on Wednesday night.

## 4. Chilled and dairy

1. Cheese, 1 block of 400 g (Tuesday grilled cheese, plus some for Friday)
2. Mozzarella, 2 balls (Friday pizza)
3. Eggs, 1 dozen
4. Milk, 2 liters
5. Butter, 1 block, if the one at home is nearly gone
6. Pizza dough, 3 chilled balls (Friday). Keep in the fridge until Friday afternoon.
7. Sliced ham, 1 small pack (Friday pizza)

## 5. Tins and jars

1. Tinned tomatoes, 3 tins of 400 g (2 for Tuesday soup, 1 for the pizza sauce)
2. Olives, 1 small jar (Friday pizza)
3. Sweetcorn, 1 small tin (Friday pizza)

## 6. Freezer

1. Peas, 1 bag of 500 g (Thursday)

## 7. For {{pick.dish}} (Wednesday)

Everything the recipe calls for that isn't already in the house. Pick it up in the matching aisles as you go, and freeze any fresh meat or fish on Sunday if it won't keep until Wednesday.

## 8. Which night uses what

| Night | Dinner | From this list |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Sheet-pan chicken | Chicken thighs, potatoes, carrots |
| Tuesday | Tomato soup and grilled cheese | Tinned tomatoes, bread, cheese, butter |
| Wednesday | {{pick.dish}} | Section 7 |
| Thursday | Sausages, mash and peas | Sausages, potatoes, peas, milk, butter |
| Friday | Pizza night | Dough, tomatoes, mozzarella, ham, peppers, olives, sweetcorn |
| Weekend | Loose | Eggs, milk, leftovers |

## 9. Pantry check

Look in the cupboard before you leave. Only buy these if you're out.

1. Flour: at least one bag, for dusting the pizza dough and anything else that comes up
2. Rice: enough for one dinner, as a backup side
3. Stock cubes: 2 for Tuesday's soup
4. Olive oil, salt and pepper: for the sheet pan and the soup
