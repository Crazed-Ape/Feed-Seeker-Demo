# {{pick.game}}: a cooperative board game for two to five players

Rebuild a storm-battered harbor town together, in about forty minutes.

## After the storm

The storm has passed, and the harbor town is in pieces. Roofs have lost their shingles, the bakery has lost its sign, and the lighthouse at the end of the pier has gone dark. The harbor lights are coming back on, one window at a time, and the neighbors are already out with ladders.

You and your friends are those neighbors. Everyone plays on the same side, with the same goal: put the town back together before the next storm arrives. When the harbor is rebuilt, everyone wins together. If the storm gets there first, nobody does.

[Photo: a game in progress, wooden tokens spread across the harbor]

## How a turn works

Each player takes on one of the town's neighbors, from the baker to the lighthouse keeper, and each one is good at something different. On your turn, you play one card and move one of your wooden tokens across the folding board. Cards repair roofs, carry supplies, clear the harbor and help other players. Between turns, a storm card comes up and undoes some of your work.

That's where the game happens. You can't fix everything alone, so the table plans out loud: who can spare a card, who's closest to the bakery, who should head for the lighthouse. Nobody is knocked out, and nobody sits a turn out.

The rules fit on a few pages, and you can learn them in one sitting. Your first game will run a little longer while everyone finds their feet.

## The last lantern

Late in the game, when the clouds are gathering again at the edge of the board, there comes a moment everyone remembers. One lantern is left, and it isn't yours to place. So you pass it across the table to the friend whose token can still reach the lighthouse in time. Nobody cheers yet. Everyone leans in. And when the light comes back on at the end of the pier, the whole table breathes out together.

That's the game we wanted to make.

## Who it's for

{{pick.game}} plays well with families, with couples and with a table of old friends, at every count from two to five. If you want a cozy evening where everyone is on the same side but there's still plenty to think about, this is it. If you like to beat your friends, there's nothing here for you to beat, except the storm.
