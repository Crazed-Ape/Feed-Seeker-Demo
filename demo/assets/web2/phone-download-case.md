# Case: the print-and-play won't download on phones

Case notes kept by {{c5.name}}. Open case. Updated as evidence comes in.

## The complaint

Three playtesters say the {{pick.game}} print-and-play download fails on their phones. On a laptop it works. Friday's playtest depends on everyone printing it first.

Exhibit A: a tester's phone screenshot of the print-and-play section, with a gray "Download failed" banner just under the download button.

## Facts

1. The file downloads and opens on a laptop. Checked three days ago, the night v3 went up, and checked again today by {{p1.name}}.
2. On at least three phones it fails. The banner sits right under the button, so the tap reached the button and something went wrong after it.
3. Nobody reported a problem with v2. It sat on the site for five weeks and was downloaded about 140 times.
4. The file on the button now is v3: 14.2 MB, 18 pages, with the new event cards on pages 11 to 13.
5. v3 went up through the upload page, and {{p1.name}} pointed the button at it by hand.

## Suspects

| # | Suspect | First impression | Status |
|---|---------|------------------|--------|
| 1 | The file is too big for phones | 14.2 MB is ordinary for a phone | Open |
| 2 | The phone layout breaks the button | Unlikely, since the banner appears; cheap to check | Open |
| 3 | The phones' PDF readers | A reader problem usually says "can't open", not "download failed" | Open |
| 4 | The file, or the way the site hands it over | v3 is the only thing that changed | Open, current favorite |

No suspect gets crossed off on a hunch. Each one is ruled out because of a piece of evidence, or it stays on the list.

## Evidence still needed

1. **The testers' own words.** Exactly what each of the three phones shows, word for word. Different wording can point at different suspects.
2. **A phone in the house.** {{p1.name}} tries the download on a phone, and notes how long it takes before anything goes wrong.
3. **The upload page's settings.** Copied from the site's admin screen: folder, size limit, allowed file types, last upload.

## Meanwhile

Friday won't wait for the investigation. While the case is open, the twelve testers should get the file some other way, so nobody turns up without event cards.

## Log

1. Case opened. Facts gathered, four suspects listed, three pieces of evidence requested.
