# Packing List: Fair Trip (Train)

Twenty items in four groups. Tick each one off twice: once into the case, once out at the hall.

## Booth

1. Banner tube. On the train it goes in the luggage rack at the end of the coach.
2. Dark tablecloth, flame-retardant, two meters.
3. Demo board of {{pick.game}}, folded, with a full set of cards.
4. Wooden tokens for the demo board, in their bag.
5. Demo lamp and its cable.
6. Campaign sign-up cards, a stack of 200, and two pens that work.
7. Power bank, charged the night before. For the train and for phones at the booth.

## Stock

8. The stock case: 36 copies of the first game.
9. Order pads, two, for shop orders and for any sales after the stock runs out.
10. Card reader for sales, charged.
11. Business cards, both of you.
12. Float for cash sales, in the zip pouch.

## Travel

13. Train tickets on both phones, and on paper.
14. Hotel Vantwell confirmation.
15. Laptops and chargers.
16. Snacks for the change. The station café closes at 14:00 on weekdays.
17. Water bottles, filled.

## Personal

18. Clothes for two fair days, one smart top each for the trade day.
19. **Socks: two extra pairs, and count them again before you close the case.**
20. Comfortable shoes, and a toothbrush each.

## Before leaving the house

Check that {{pick.dog}} is not inside the stock case.
