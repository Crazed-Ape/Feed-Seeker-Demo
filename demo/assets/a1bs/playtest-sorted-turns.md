## Theme 1: Turn Order

Seven of twelve comments are about the order of a turn.

| Group | Count | Testers |
|---|---|---|
| Unsure whether weather comes before or after actions | 4 | 1, 3, 8, 10 |
| Confused by switching orders mid-game | 3 | 5, 6, 7 |

The two printed sources give two different orders:

1. Rulebook, page 4: take two actions, draw weather, refill the market.
2. Reference card: draw weather, take two actions, refill the market.

Notes:

1. Table 2 switched in summer after someone read page 4 aloud (tester 6). It had the most confused comments, the lowest clarity and the longest game.
2. Tables 1 and 3 each stuck to one order and both finished within 5 minutes of the box.
