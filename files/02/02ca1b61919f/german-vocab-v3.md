# German for the Fair: Vocabulary

Version 3 · Complete: lessons 1 to 5

## How to read the hints

The hints are sound-alikes, not exact spellings. Capital letters show where the weight goes.

1. **ö** is close to "er" with rounded lips, written here as "er".
2. **ü** is "ee" with rounded lips, written here as "ue".
3. **ch** after i or e is a soft hiss, written here as "kh".

| # | German | Sounds like | Meaning |

## Lesson 4: numbers and prices

### Numbers

| Number | German | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | eins, zwei, drei, vier, fünf | ines, tsvy, dry, feer, fuenf |
| 10 | zehn | tsayn |
| 20 | zwanzig | TSVAN-tsikh |
| 30 | dreißig | DRY-sikh |
| 40 | vierzig | FEER-tsikh |
| 50 | fünfzig | FUENF-tsikh |
| 100 | hundert | HOON-dert |

### Prices

| # | German | Sounds like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Was kostet das? | vahs KOSS-tet dahs | What does it cost? |
| 2 | Das kostet fünfundzwanzig. | dahs KOSS-tet FUENF-oont-tsvan-tsikh | It costs twenty-five. |
| 3 | Ab welcher Stückzahl gibt es einen Rabatt? | ahp VEL-kher SHTUECK-tsahl gipt es EYE-nen rah-BAHT | From what quantity is there a discount? |
| 4 | Haben Sie eine Visitenkarte? | HAH-ben zee EYE-nuh vi-ZEE-ten-kar-tuh | Do you have a business card? |

Ones come before tens: 25 is **fünfundzwanzig**, "five-and-twenty", and 42 is **zweiundvierzig**. Wait for the end, and write it down.

## Lesson 5: small talk and goodbyes

| # | German | Sounds like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Woher kommen Sie? | vo-HAIR KOM-men zee | Where are you from? |
| 2 | Ich komme aus Marrowby. Es liegt am Meer. | ikh KOM-muh ows Marrowby. es leekt ahm mair | I'm from Marrowby. It's by the sea. |
| 3 | Wir sind ein kleines Spielestudio. | veer zint ine KLY-nes SHPEE-luh-shtoo-dee-oh | We're a small game studio. |
| 4 | Hier ist meine Karte. | heer ist MY-nuh KAR-tuh | Here's my card. |
| 5 | Schön, Sie kennenzulernen. | shern zee KEN-nen-tsoo-lair-nen | Nice to meet you. |
| 6 | Bis morgen! | bis MOR-gen | See you tomorrow! |

**Karte** is both a business card and a card in a game, so line 4 tends to get a smile at a board-game booth. A playing card, to be exact, is a **Spielkarte** (SHPEEL-kar-tuh).

## Sie and du

1. **Sie** is polite: strangers, suppliers, anyone selling you something.
2. **du** is for friends. At game fairs people often move to it quickly.
3. Start with **Sie** and follow the other person's lead.
