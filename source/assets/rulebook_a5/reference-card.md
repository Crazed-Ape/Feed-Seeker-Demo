## Front · Your turn, in four steps

1. **Move:** up to two docks along the path, either way, or stay.
2. **Take or trade:** take one card from the tide line, or swap one card with a neighbor at your dock.
3. **Work:** play cards that match empty spaces on the task at your dock. One shell per card. Five shells finish a task.
4. **Rest:** more than five cards? Discard down to five.

Talk at any time. It's the whole game.

## Back · When everyone has had a turn

1. **Clear the tide line:** discard any cards left in it.
2. **Bring in the tide:** reveal five Tide cards from the deck to make a new tide line. Can't reveal five? The game ends (see page 10).
3. **Waves:** for each new card with a wave, remove one shell from the unfinished task at the dock it shows. Finished tasks are safe, and so is the Net Mender's dock.
4. **Next round:** the player on the left of this round's first player goes first.

Count the deck. It's your clock.
