# First Eruption: Test-Run Steps

Do these in order. Each step has a check, and you only move on when the check is true. The grown-up pours. The scientist counts.

## Recipe at a glance

2 tbsp baking soda, a squirt of dish soap, 5 drops red coloring, then half a cup of vinegar all at once.

## Before you start

1. The mountain is on its tray, with the bottle's opening clear.
2. The bucket and two old towels are next to the patio table.
3. The bottle's lid is in a drawer, not anywhere near the volcano.

## The steps

1. Put the tray on the patio table and put on goggles. Check: both pairs are on.
2. Spoon 2 tablespoons of baking soda into the bottle (a rolled-up piece of paper makes a good funnel). Check: the powder is at the bottom, not stuck in the neck.
3. Add a squirt of dish soap and five drops of red coloring. Check: you can see red at the bottom.
4. Measure half a cup of vinegar into a jug. Check: it reaches the half-cup line.
5. Count down from three, pour all the vinegar in at once, then step back. Check: everyone is an arm's length from the top.
6. Watch and count. Check: you've written down how high the foam went and for how many seconds.
7. Tip what's left into the bucket and rinse the bottle. Check: the bottle is empty before any second go.

## Safety reminders

1. Goggles stay on until step 7 is done.
2. Nobody puts a lid on the bottle once the vinegar is in.
3. Nobody tastes the lava, not even a little.
