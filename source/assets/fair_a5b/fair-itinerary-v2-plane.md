# Fair Trip: Itinerary, Version 2 (Plane)

Fly out on Wednesday morning, one night at Hotel Vantwell, home on Thursday around 22:30.

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| Flights | Two people, return, €522. Out on Wednesday at 6:50, home on Thursday at 19:30 |

## Setup in 45 minutes

The booth kit comes out of one case in this order, so nothing waits for anything else:

1. Cloth.
2. Board.
3. Lamp.
4. Tokens.
5. Cards.
6. Banner, last.
