## Correction

Version 1 gave {{pick.printer_b}}'s freight as 1,040. That figure is their rate for each extra pallet beyond 1,500 copies. Their freight for this run is 1,400 (page 2, second table). The total, the all-in price per copy and the difference below are corrected to match.

## The difference

1. {{pick.printer_b}} costs 1,030 more, about 6.3%.
2. For that, it offers two weeks less wait and a thicker box (2 mm board instead of 1.5 mm).
3. Both ask the same 30% deposit.
4. Both quoted every part of the spec in one quote, so no line is missing on either side.

## Not in the quotes

1. Whether each printer usually keeps its promised dates.
2. How the cards and wooden tokens tend to turn out.
3. Whether boxes tend to arrive undamaged.

These are with {{c2.name}}, who is checking both printers' records with other small studios.

## Reading so far

On money alone, {{pick.printer_a}}, and by a wider margin than version 1 showed. The choice stays open until the track records are in.
