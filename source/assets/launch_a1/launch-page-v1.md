# A Warm Hug in a Box

## After the storm

The wind has finally dropped, the rain has wandered off out to sea, and the little harbor town is a mess. Roofs are missing their shingles, the bakery has lost its sign, and the lighthouse at the end of the pier has gone dark. But the kettles are still warm, the neighbors are still here, and the amber harbor lights are flickering back on, one cozy window at a time.

Welcome to {{pick.game}}, the cozy cooperative board game where you and your friends put a whole town back together, one heartwarming repair at a time.

## A cozy game for cozy evenings

{{pick.game}} is for two to five players and takes about forty minutes, which is just long enough for a pot of tea and a second slice of cake. Nobody plays against anybody. You share your cards, you share your plans, and you share the happy groan when the clouds roll back in. Everyone wins together, or the storm does. It's a game sprinkled with friendship, from the first card to the very last lantern.

On your turn, you play a card, lend a hand to a neighbor, and move your wooden tokens across the folding board to wherever the town needs you most. The baker needs flour. The net-mender needs rope. Somebody really should check on the lighthouse keeper. Every turn feels like tucking a blanket around someone you love.

Every so often a storm card turns up and the weather tries to undo all your good work. That's when the talking starts. Who can spare a card? Who's closest to the bakery? Before long you'll be planning together like old neighbors leaning on a garden fence.

## The last lantern

Late in the game, when the clouds are gathering again at the edge of the board, there comes a moment everyone remembers. One lantern is left, and it isn't yours to place. So you pass it across the table to the friend whose token can still reach the lighthouse in time. Nobody cheers yet. Everyone leans in. And when the light comes back on at the end of the pier, the whole table breathes out together.

That's the game we wanted to make.
