# Exhibitor Pack: The Board Game Fair in {{pick.fair_city}}

Welcome, exhibitors. This pack covers everything you need to book, set up and run your space. Please read sections 6 to 9 before you arrive.

## 1. The fair at a glance

1. Trade day: Wednesday, 10:30 to 18:00. Open to shops, distributors and press with a trade badge.
2. Public day: Thursday, 10:00 to 17:00. Open to all visitors.
3. One hall, two entrances: the main entrance on the main aisle, and the side entrance on the east wall.

## 2. Who exhibits

Publishers, independent studios, accessory makers and game shops. Independent studios of up to three people may book a table in the indie row.

The visitor map counts the main entrance as the busy one. Most visitors use it, and the side entrance is quieter on both days.

## 5. Table partners

Exhibitors who book a full indie table may ask the fair office for a partner to share it. Each partner pays half and has half the table: one meter each.

## 6. Setup and pack-down

1. Setup: Wednesday, 8:00 to 10:30. The hall opens to trade visitors at 10:30 and no setup is allowed after that.
2. The loading bay is at the north wall. Vehicles may stop for 20 minutes.
3. Pack-down starts when the hall closes at 17:00 on Thursday, and must be finished by 19:00.
4. Nothing may be packed away before closing time on either day.

## 7. Power

1. Booth types A to E have their own socket.
2. Each indie table has one double socket under the table, shared by the table's partners. Partners agree its use between themselves.
3. Extension leads must be of the safe, multi-socket type with a switch. No chains of adaptors.
4. The hall lighting is low over the indie row and the east side. Small lamps are welcome.

## 8. Walls, banners and cloths

1. Corner and inline booths have a back wall, light gray, 2.4 m high, with a top rail for hanging banners.
2. Indie tables have no wall. Banners on stands up to 1.8 m may stand behind the table.
3. Indie tables come with the fair's orange skirting cloth, floor-length, on three sides. Exhibitors may lay their own cloth over it, provided it is flame-retardant.
4. Nothing may be fixed to the walls with nails, screws or tape that marks.

## 9. Safety

1. Aisles must stay clear at all times.
2. All materials must be flame-retardant.
3. Food and drink samples need the fair office's written permission.

## 10. Badges, parking and deliveries

1. Collect exhibitor badges at the north wall desk from 7:45 on Wednesday.
2. Deliveries to the hall are accepted on Tuesday from 14:00 to 18:00 only.
3. The fair's north gate is the closest to the hotels on the north side.

2. Cancellations within 30 days: no refund.
