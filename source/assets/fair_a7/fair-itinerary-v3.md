## Correction: sockets

Version 2 said coach 6 has a socket at every pair of seats. That is true of the newer trains only. On Tuesdays and Thursdays this route still runs the older trains for the first leg.

1. On the older coaches, sockets are only at the four tables at each end of the coach. Seats 41 and 42 are not at those tables.
2. Coach 4 is the café coach. Its counter seats have sockets.
3. The second train after the change is the newer type, with a socket at every seat.
