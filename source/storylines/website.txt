▫️ **Side by side**

1. **Size:** v2 is 11.8 MB, v3 is 14.2 MB. Both fine.
2. **How the site describes them:** both go out as "PDF document". Fine.
3. **Name:** v2 is `print-and-play-v2.pdf`. v3 is `print and play v3.pdf`. There it is.

▫️ **How a space breaks a phone**

A web address can't contain a real space. A laptop browser quietly repairs one: it writes the space as `%20` and asks for the right file. The phone browsers our testers use are less forgiving. They read the address up to the first space and ask the site for a file called `print`. No such file exists, so the site sends back its "page not found" page and the phone reports "Download failed". The third tester's phone saved that "not found" page under the PDF's name, which is why the file "can't be opened".

▫️ **My part in it**

I checked the upload three days ago the way a laptop does, saw it work, and stopped there. A check on a phone would have caught it that night.

▫️ **The fix**

The quick fix is a rename. The lasting fix is making sure the upload page can't let it happen again.\
