=== m meals.16 thread=meals-main side=a from=c1 to=p3 turn=t7 part=1
{{c1.emoji}} **{{c1.name}}** · {{date}} · {{time}}

▫️ **How many**

This batch makes about twelve small pancakes, four each for three people. You need 200 g of flour, 2 eggs, 300 ml of milk, 1 tablespoon of sugar, 2 teaspoons of baking powder, a pinch of salt and a little butter for the pan. The eggs and milk came from Sunday's shop and the flour passed the pantry check.

▫️ **How to make them**

Do these in order, and don't go on until the check is true. {{p1.name}} stays at the stove the whole time; you're in charge of the spatula.

1. Wash your hands. Check: hands are dry before you touch the flour.
2. In a big bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Check: no white lumps of baking powder left.
3. {{p1.name}} cracks the eggs into the milk, and you whisk them together. Check: no bits of shell.
4. Pour the wet bowl into the dry bowl and stir until it's just mixed. Check: a few small lumps are fine, so stop stirring.
5. {{p1.name}} heats the pan on medium with a little butter. Check: a drop of batter sizzles gently and doesn't smoke.
6. Pour one small ladle for each pancake. Check: they don't touch each other.
7. Flip when the bubbles on top pop and stay open, after about 2 minutes. Check: the edges look dry.
8. Cook the second side for about 1 minute. Check: golden underneath, then onto the plate.

Turn the pan handle in toward the wall so nobody bumps it.\
↳ `Y4`
