▫️ **Saltmarrow, at dusk**

The fishing town of Saltmarrow sits in the elbow of a gray bay, all crooked roofs and drying nets. Gulls argue on every chimney. Out on Gullstone Point the lighthouse stands like a finger raised to ask a question, and for three nights now it hasn't answered. The lamp is dark.

1. The old Keeper climbed the lighthouse three days ago and hasn't come down.
